Short Bio
I currently work on building TensorRT Compiler at Nvidia.

Previously, I worked at Amazon as a backend compiler engineer, focusing on scheduling, engine balancing, dependency analysis and allocation for Trainium devices. I also worked in AWS Deep Engines Org out of Santa Clara building on inference optimizations for GPU workloads for Sage Maker and Bedrock products. Prior to this, I worked with Synopsys and Xilinx working on placement and routing algorithm optimizations.

I graduated from University of Cincinnati in Electrical Engineering. I did my bachelors in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani, Hyderabad Campus.
